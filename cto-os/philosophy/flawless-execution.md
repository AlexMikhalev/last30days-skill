# Operating Philosophy: Flawless Execution (Delivery)

(Loosely inspired by "The 4 Disciplines of Execution" style systems.)

## The aim
Keep delivery solid while making space for high-leverage AI work.

## Principles
1) **Wildly Important Goals (WIGs)**: 1–3 outcomes per week.
2) **Lead measures**: track controllable actions (PRs merged, evals run, demos published).
3) **Scoreboard**: keep scorecards updated weekly.
4) **Cadence**: weekly review is non-negotiable.

## Guardrails
- No more than 5 active projects.
- Every project has: DRI, next actions, risk.
- Incidents get written postmortems.

## Marketing as execution
- A feature is not “done” until it is **marketed** (post/demo/docs).
